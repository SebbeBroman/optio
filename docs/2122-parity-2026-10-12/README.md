## Why 2122 was open

`2122` is a maximization MILP (855 cols, 1060 rows, 257 integer) that is
essentially solved at the root: the optimum is 0.006 % above the LP relaxation.
It exposed four separate defects, each fixed generically:

1. **No node dual bound.** `Simplex::finish` evaluated the Lagrangian term
   `min_{lo≤z≤hi} rc_j·z` over *both* sign branches of the reduced-cost error
   interval. On a column with a one-sided infinite bound and a reduced cost that
   merely rounds to the wrong side of zero, the unbounded endpoint was chosen and
   the whole bound became −∞ → `dual_bound = None`. Every B&B node then carried
   `bound = -inf`, so best-bound search degenerated and never pruned. The term is
   now evaluated from the basis: zero for basic columns, and the basis-implied
   sign at the finite endpoint for nonbasic columns (the logical-column `y`
   projection is no longer needed). Root `dualBound` for `2122` went from `null`
   to −187 604.067.

2. **Degenerate cycling.** One node LP cycled in phase 2 for >400 000 no-progress
   iterations. Bland's rule requires *both* halves; `price` already picked the
   smallest-index entering column, but the ratio test picked the largest pivot
   among near-ties. The leaving variable is now the smallest-index variable among
   the minimum-ratio ties when Bland is active.

3. **Weak branching where learned pseudocosts are noise.** On `2122` the learned
   gains are small and misleading; strong branching with a 3-candidate shortlist,
   `depth ≤ 6`, 24-probe cap reached only bound −187 609.24 over 30 s. The
   shortlist is now 8, the depth limit 40, and the probe cap effectively removed
   (the per-node probe deadline still bounds work). This is the change that takes
   `2122` to a closed bound in 3817 nodes.

4. **Over-strict box check on large-valued columns.** After a successful LP, a
   point that violated a branch bound by 1.2e‑5 (3e‑9 relative on a value of
   4000) was downgraded to `Numerical` and the node abandoned. `respects_bounds`
   now scales its tolerance with the bound magnitude. (`price` also refactors and
   re-prices once before declaring a numerically dependent basis.)

A small resource change accompanies these: the `feasibility_pump`/`dive`
fallback share dropped from `min(remaining·0.2, 2 s)` to `min(remaining·0.1, 1 s)`,
freeing ~1 s for the proof search on hard feasibility cases. `planning-s` also
improved to 679.35.
